# Ask Uncle Mike — YouTube series (slice 1)

## Promise
Show it. Say it in regular words. Leave people able to do the next step without a translator.

## Episode shape (keep this short on camera)

1. **The question** — title is the question a real person would type.
2. **Why it matters** — 20 seconds, no speech.
3. **Show the hands** — do the thing. Talk while doing.
4. **The one rule** — the piece that stops most people from failing.
5. **Bags at the door** — drop extra theory. Only what this task needs.
6. **Check** — how you know you did it right.
7. **Ask Uncle Mike** — one line invite for the next question.

Target length: 4–8 minutes for a skill. 8–12 only if the hands need time.

## Title pattern

`Ask Uncle Mike: How to tie a tie`
`Ask Uncle Mike: How to check your oil`
`Ask Uncle Mike: What a phishing text actually looks like`

No clickbait numbers. No “you won’t believe.”

## Labels on camera and in the notes

- **Fact** — checkable.
- **My path** — what I did, what I weighed, what my labs did. Not your doctor.
- **Opinion** — labeled so it cannot hide as news.
- **Not legal / not medical / not tax advice** — one line in description when the subject touches those.
